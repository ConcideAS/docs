title: Admin panel
description: Manage your organization's settings, users, integrations, and shared libraries.

The admin panel is available to organization administrators. Access it by clicking **Admin panel** in the sidebar.

From here you can configure how Saga works for your entire organization — from AI model settings and integrations to shared prompt libraries and user management.
