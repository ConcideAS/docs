Solo workspaces include self-serve billing for subscribers who manage a smaller team.

## Billing portal

The billing portal lets Solo subscribers manage payment methods, invoices, and subscription status without contacting support. It is self-serve, so everything is accessible directly from your account.

## Seats and billing roles

Solo subscriptions support up to 5 users sharing one subscription. The person who purchases the subscription is automatically assigned the Billing Admin role.

The Billing Admin can manage the subscription, update payment methods, and download invoices.

If you need to transfer the Billing Admin role, contact [support@sagalegal.io](mailto:support@sagalegal.io).
