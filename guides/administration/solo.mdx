# Overview

Solo is Saga's self-serve plan for individual practitioners and small teams of up to 5 users. You manage your own subscription, seats, and invoices directly from the billing portal, with no need to contact support for day-to-day account changes.

## Billing portal

The billing portal is where Solo subscribers manage payment methods, download invoices, and check subscription status. It is self-serve, so everything is accessible directly from your account.

## Seats and billing roles

A Solo subscription covers up to 5 users sharing one subscription. The person who purchases the subscription is automatically assigned the **Billing Admin** role.

The Billing Admin can:

- Manage the subscription and update payment methods
- Download invoices
- Add users to the workspace from the **Licensed Users** section of the admin panel (see [Admin panel](/guides/administration/admin-panel))

To transfer the Billing Admin role to someone else, contact [support@sagalegal.io](mailto:support@sagalegal.io).

## When you outgrow Solo

If your team grows past 5 users or you need firm-wide controls like automatic user provisioning and organizational sources, consider [Enterprise](/guides/administration/enterprise). Contact [support@sagalegal.io](mailto:support@sagalegal.io) to discuss an upgrade.
