The **GDPR Compliance Assessment (End-to-End)** workflow is a Saga Lab template that automates a full data protection audit. It takes your documents, analyzes them against GDPR and LOPDGDD requirements, and produces a professional report with a compliance score, risk matrix, and prioritized recommendations.

It is designed for legal and compliance teams that need to evaluate an organization's compliance posture in a structured and repeatable way.

<Note>
This workflow and the associated prompt template are available in Spanish and Portuguese. The workflow runs its analysis in Spanish, and the prompt templates are available under the names **Cumplimiento del RGPD** (Spanish) and **Conformidade com o RGPD** (Portuguese) in the Saga Lab library.
</Note>

## When to use it

Use this workflow when you need to:

- Audit data protection documentation for a client or your own organization
- Evaluate data processing agreements (DPAs) against GDPR Article 28
- Generate a compliance report with identified risks and recommendations
- Produce an objective compliance score for presentation to a committee or client

## Getting started

Open the [workflow library](/guides/workflows/library) and search for **Evaluación de Cumplimiento RGPD** in the **Saga Lab** section. Select the template to preview its steps before running, or duplicate it into your personal collection to customize it.

## What you need to prepare

Before running the workflow, gather whatever data protection documents you have available. The more documents you provide, the more thorough the analysis will be — but you can run it with whatever you have.

Documents you can include:

- Privacy policy
- Record of processing activities (ROPA)
- Data processing agreement (DPA)
- Data protection impact assessment (DPIA)
- Security breach protocol
- Related resolutions or reports

<Note>
Documents don't need to be complete. If any document is missing, the analysis runs on the available information and flags what it could not evaluate.
</Note>

## Workflow steps

The workflow runs eight sequential steps. Each step feeds its output to the next, building a progressively more detailed analysis.

### 1. Document upload

Upload one or more data protection documents. This is the only step that requires your direct input — the rest of the workflow runs automatically.

### 2. Standard data protection audit

Runs a structured [grid review](/guides/grid-review) that extracts key information from each document:

| Column | What it extracts |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Personal data** | Explicit data types (name, email, phone, biometric data, IP) |
| **Data subjects** | Categories of affected individuals (clients, employees, web users, suppliers) |
| **Purpose** | Processing purpose (client management, marketing, labor control) |
| **Legal basis** | Legal basis for processing (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest) |
| **GDPR principles** | Applicable principles per Article 5 |
| **Transfers** | International transfers and mechanisms used |
| **Security** | Security measures implemented (encryption, access control, backups) |
| **Retention** | Data retention periods and criteria |
| **Risks** | Identified risks (unauthorized access, data loss, misuse) |
| **GDPR obligations** | Legal obligations mentioned (ROPA, DPA, DPIA, breach notification) |
| **Non-compliance** | Explicit non-compliance findings |
| **Sanctions** | Amounts, infringement types, and competent authority |

### 3. Compliance analysis

Analyzes the grid review results and generates a report with:

- GDPR compliance level (high, medium, or low)
- Main risks identified by category
- Detected non-compliance findings
- Prioritized recommendations (high, medium, low)

### 4. Legal impact assessment

For each identified risk, classifies the legal impact as high, medium, or low — considering the type of data involved, the absence or weakness of security measures, and possible GDPR principle violations.

### 5. Professional compliance report

Drafts a structured report with an executive summary, compliance level, principal risks, detected non-compliance findings, and recommendations. The report uses clear legal language targeted at legal and compliance teams.

### 6. Risk matrix

Generates a professional risk matrix (impact vs. probability) that classifies each risk as critical, high, medium, or low. It includes the top three priority risks and distinguishes between regulatory and operational risks.

### 7. Compliance score

Calculates a score from 0 to 100 based on the detected non-compliance findings:

| Score | Classification |
|-------|---------------|
| 80–100 | High compliance |
| 50–79 | Medium compliance |
| Below 50 | Low compliance |

The result includes the factors that most reduce the score and quick wins for improvement.

### 8. Executive summary

Produces an executive dashboard with the overall compliance status, key risks, risk distribution, and a traffic-light indicator (green, yellow, red). It is designed for direct presentation to committees or clients.

## GDPR compliance prompt

In addition to the workflow, the [prompt library](/guides/chat/prompt-library) includes a **GDPR compliance** template in the Saga Lab section (available in Spanish as **Cumplimiento del RGPD** and in Portuguese as **Conformidade com o RGPD**). This prompt evaluates a data processing agreement against GDPR Article 28 requirements and generates a compliance table with comments and improvement suggestions.

Use this prompt when you need a quick review of an individual DPA in chat, without running the full workflow.
