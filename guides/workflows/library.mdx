---
title: Workflow library
description: Browse and reuse pre-built workflows for common legal tasks.
---

Browse pre-built workflows in the **Workflow library**. These cover common legal tasks and can be used as-is or customized to fit your needs.

Admins can create and share workflows across the organization. See [Admin panel](/guides/admin-panel) for details.

<Tip>
Save workflows you use frequently to the library so your team can reuse them.
</Tip>
