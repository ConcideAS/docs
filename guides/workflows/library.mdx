---
title: Workflow library
---

The workflow library gives you access to pre-built workflow templates and lets you save your own for reuse.

## Browse templates

Open the library from the Workflows page. Templates are organized into three categories:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Personal** | Workflows you've created and saved |
| **Organization** | Workflows shared across your organization |
| **SagaLab** | Curated templates created by Saga |

Use the search bar to find templates by name, or filter by category.

## Favorite workflows

Click the star icon on any template to add it to your favorites for quick access.

## Save a workflow

After building a workflow, save it as a template:

1. Click **Save** in the workflow builder.
2. Give the template a name.
3. The workflow is saved to your **Personal** templates.

## Duplicate a workflow

To create a copy of an existing workflow — for example, to modify it without changing the original — click **Duplicate** on the template.

## Share with your organization

To make a personal workflow available to everyone in your organization, click **Request to make public**. An administrator can review and approve it, after which it appears in the **Organization** category for all users.

<Note>
Organization-wide workflows are managed by admins in the [admin panel](/guides/admin/libraries).
</Note>
