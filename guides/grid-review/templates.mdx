---
title: Templates
description: Use pre-built or custom templates to quickly set up grid reviews.
---

Use pre-built templates for common review types instead of defining questions from scratch. Templates provide a starting set of columns tailored to specific review scenarios.

## Use a template

To use a template, click **Templates** when creating a new grid review and select one.

## Custom templates

Admins can create custom templates for your organization. See [Admin panel](/guides/admin-panel) for details.
