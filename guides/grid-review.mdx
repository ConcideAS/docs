---
title: Grid review
description: Review documents systematically with AI-powered question grids.
---

Grid review lets you analyze multiple documents against a set of questions. Each document becomes a row, each question a column, and the AI fills in answers — giving you a structured overview of your documents.

## Create a grid review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a matter">
    Go to **Matters** and open the matter containing your documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a grid review">
    Click the **Grid review** tab, then click **New grid review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    Define the questions you want answered for each document. Each question becomes a column in the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documents">
    Select which documents from the matter's knowledge base to include. Each document becomes a row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the review">
    Click **Run** and the AI analyzes each document against your questions, filling in the grid.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Templates

Use pre-built templates for common review types instead of defining questions from scratch. Templates provide a starting set of columns tailored to specific review scenarios.

To use a template, click **Templates** when creating a new grid review and select one.

Admins can create custom templates for your organization. See [Admin panel](/guides/admin-panel) for details.

## Work with the grid

### Edit columns

- **Add** a column by clicking the **+** button.
- **Edit** a column's question by clicking the column header.
- **Delete** a column by right-clicking the header and selecting **Delete**.

### Edit rows

- **Add** documents as rows from the matter's knowledge base.
- **Remove** a row by right-clicking and selecting **Delete**.

### Citations

Each AI-generated answer includes citations. Click a citation to view the referenced passage in the **Instruments sheet** side panel.

## Export

Click **Export** to download the grid as an Excel file. The export includes all questions, documents, and AI-generated answers.
