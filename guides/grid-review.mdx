---
title: Grid review
description: Review documents systematically with AI-powered question grids.
---

Grid review lets you analyze multiple documents against a set of questions. Each document becomes a row, each question a column, and the AI fills in answers — giving you a structured overview of your documents.

## Create a grid review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a project">
    Go to **Projects** and open the project containing your documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a grid review">
    Click the **Grid review** tab, then click **New grid review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    Define the questions you want answered for each document. Each question becomes a column in the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documents">
    Select which documents from the project's knowledge base to include. Each document becomes a row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the review">
    Click **Run** and the AI analyzes each document against your questions, filling in the grid.
  </Step>
</Steps>
