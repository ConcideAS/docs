---
title: Word plugin
---

Saga offers a Microsoft Word add-in that brings AI capabilities into your Word workflow. Use the plugin to interact with Saga without leaving your document editor.

## Install the plugin

The Word plugin is available as a Microsoft Office add-in. Contact your organization's administrator for installation instructions specific to your setup.

## Sign in

The plugin uses a separate authentication flow:

1. Open the Saga add-in in Word.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Click the magic link sent to your inbox to verify.
4. Once verified, the plugin connects to your Saga account.

## Features

The Word plugin supports AI chat features, allowing you to ask questions and work with Saga's assistants directly within your Word documents.

<Note>
Chat history from the Word plugin is tracked separately from your web chats. You can filter your chat history by source (web or Word plugin) in the main Saga interface.
</Note>
