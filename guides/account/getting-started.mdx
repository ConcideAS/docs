---
title: Getting started
---

This guide covers how to access Saga, sign in for the first time, and find your way around the interface.

## Sign in

Go to your organization's Saga URL or [app.saga.com](https://app.saga.com). Saga supports two sign-in methods:

- **Email** — Enter your email address and click the magic link sent to your inbox
- **Microsoft** — Sign in with your organization's Microsoft account (Azure AD)

Your administrator determines which sign-in methods are available for your organization.

## SMS verification

If your organization requires SMS verification, you'll be prompted to enter a code sent to your phone number after signing in with email.

## Navigate the interface

Saga's sidebar gives you access to every feature:

| Section | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Chat** | AI conversations with optional document context |
| **Projects** | Workspaces for organizing cases, documents, and reviews |
| **Legal research** | Search across legal databases |
| **Translate** | Translate documents and text |
| **Workflows** | Visual automation builder |
| **Admin panel** | Organization settings (admins only) |

## First-time setup

After your first sign-in, Saga may guide you through an onboarding wizard to personalize your experience. You can:

- Set your preferred AI assistant
- Choose your interface language
- Take a quick tour of key features

All of these preferences can be changed later in your [settings](/guides/account/settings).
