---
title: Exporting conversations
description: Export chat conversations as Word documents.
---

You can export a chat conversation as a Word document (.docx) to save a record of the exchange, share it with colleagues who don't have Saga access, or include it in your case files.

## Export a conversation

1. Open the chat you want to export.
2. Click the **Export to Word** button in the chat header.
3. The conversation downloads as a .docx file.

## Export a single message

To export just one message instead of the full conversation:

1. Hover over the message.
2. Click the **Export to Word** action.
3. The message downloads as a .docx file.

## What's included

The exported document contains the full conversation thread, including:

- Your messages and the AI's responses
- Citations referenced in the responses
- The conversation title and timestamp
