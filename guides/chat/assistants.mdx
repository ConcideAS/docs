---
title: Assistants
---

Saga lets you choose which AI assistant handles your conversation. Switch between different AI models or use custom assistants that your organization has configured for specific tasks.

## Basic assistants

Basic assistants correspond to different AI models (such as GPT, Claude, Mistral, or DeepSeek). Each model has different strengths — some are faster, others are better at complex reasoning.

To select a basic assistant, click the **assistant selector** at the bottom of the chat input and choose from the available models.

## Custom assistants

Custom assistants are pre-configured by your organization's administrators with specific instructions, behavior, and capabilities. For example, your firm might have a custom assistant tailored for contract analysis or regulatory compliance.

Custom assistants appear in a separate tab in the assistant selector.

<Note>
Custom assistants are created and managed by admins in the [admin panel](/guides/admin/libraries). If you need a custom assistant for your team, contact your administrator.
</Note>

## Default assistant

Saga uses a default assistant when you start a new conversation. This default can be set at two levels:

- **Workspace default** — Set by an admin, applies to all users in the organization
- **User default** — Set in your personal [settings](/guides/account/settings), overrides the workspace default

## Thinking mode

Some models support a thinking or reasoning mode, where the AI shows its step-by-step reasoning process in an expandable section before the final answer. This is useful for complex legal questions where you want to see how the AI arrived at its conclusion.

You can skip thinking mode for faster responses when you don't need the detailed reasoning.
