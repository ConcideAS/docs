---
title: Theme and branding
description: Customize your Saga instance with your organization's logo and brand assets.
---

Personalize your Saga instance to match your organization's visual identity. Access theme settings from **Admin panel > Preferences > Theme**.

## Branding options

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Logo** | Your organization's logo, displayed in the sidebar and login page |
| **Favicon** | The browser tab icon |
| **Brand assets** | Additional visual elements used across the interface |

Upload your assets in SVG or PNG format for the best results. Saga supports separate assets for light and dark themes.
