title: Usage
---

The usage page gives administrators visibility into how Saga is being used across the organization. Access it from **Admin panel > Organization > Usage**.

## What's tracked

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **AI messages** | Number of AI chat messages sent across the organization |
| **Storage** | Total document storage used in knowledge bases and organizational sources |

Use these metrics to understand adoption patterns, plan capacity, and ensure your organization stays within its usage allocation.
