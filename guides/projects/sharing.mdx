---
title: Sharing and permissions
description: Share projects with colleagues and control who can view or edit.
---

Projects can be shared with other users in your organization. When you share a project, collaborators get access to the knowledge base, chats, and grid reviews inside it.

## Permission levels

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Owner** | Full control — edit, share, delete the project |
| **Co-owner** | Edit, share, and manage the project. Can leave or delete |
| **Edit** | Add and edit documents, create chats and grid reviews |
| **Read-only** | View documents, chats, and grid reviews without making changes |

## Share a project

1. Open the project you want to share.
2. Click the **Share** button.
3. Search for a colleague by email address.
4. Select a permission level for them.
5. Click **Add**.

You can change permissions or remove users at any time from the same share dialog.

## Share a chat

Within a shared project, individual chats are private by default. To share a chat with all project members:

1. Open the chat inside the project.
2. Click the **Share** button in the chat header.
3. Confirm sharing — all project members will be able to view the conversation and its referenced documents.

A system message appears in the chat when it is shared.

## Filter project chats

In a shared project, you can filter the chat list to see:

- **All** chats you have access to
- **Shared** chats that have been made visible to project members
- **Private** chats that only you can see
