---
title: Document viewer
---

Saga opens documents in a side panel called the **instruments sheet**. The viewer appears when you click a citation in chat, open a document from the knowledge base, or click a cell reference in a grid review.

## Supported viewers

Saga renders documents in their native format:

| File type | Viewer features |
|-----------|----------------|
| **PDF** | Zoom, in-document search, citation highlighting |
| **DOCX** | Rendered HTML view with citation highlighting |
| **XLS / XLSX** | Spreadsheet viewer |
| **EML / MSG** | Parsed email with from, to, cc, bcc, subject, body, and attachments |

## Navigate citations

When the AI cites a passage in its response, the citation links directly to the relevant section of the document. Click the citation number in the chat message to:

1. Open the document in the side panel.
2. Scroll to and highlight the referenced passage.

This works for both PDF and DOCX documents.

## Viewer controls

- **Zoom** — Increase or decrease the document zoom level (PDF)
- **Search** — Find text within the currently open document (PDF)
- **Close** — Click the close button or click outside the panel to dismiss it

## Where the viewer opens from

The document viewer is available throughout Saga:

- **Chat citations** — Click any numbered citation in an AI response
- **Knowledge base** — Click a document name in the knowledge base file list
- **Grid review cells** — Click a citation badge inside a grid review answer
- **Legal research results** — Preview a search result before adding it to a project
