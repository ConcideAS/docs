Chat is where you work with AI in Saga. Ask questions about your documents, draft content, analyze materials, or run research — all through a natural language conversation. The AI draws on whatever context you give it and cites its sources so you can verify every answer.

## Starting a conversation

Click **Chat** in the sidebar to open a new conversation. Type your question and press **Enter** to send.

You can also start a chat from inside a project. When you do, the AI automatically has access to that project's knowledge base — every active document becomes context for the conversation without you needing to attach anything.

## How context works

The AI's answers depend on what information it has access to. In a **project chat**, that context comes from the project's knowledge base. In a **global chat** (outside any project), you provide context by attaching files, selecting organizational sources, or enabling web search. You can also combine multiple sources in a single conversation.

For a full breakdown of how to control what the AI sees, see [Context and sources](/guides/chat/context-and-sources).

## Citations

When the AI references a document, it includes inline citations — small numbered chips linked to the specific passage it drew from. Hover over a citation to see the original excerpt. Click it to open the source document in the side panel, scrolled to the relevant section.

Citations work for project documents, legal research results, and web sources. They're how you verify what the AI is telling you without hunting through files yourself.

## Message actions

Hover over any message to see its available actions:

- **Copy** the message content to your clipboard
- **Regenerate** a new response to the same question
- **Export to Word** to download the message as a .docx file
- **Edit** your original message to get a revised response
- **Like / Dislike** to give feedback on the response quality
- **Save to Prompt Library** to reuse a prompt you've written
- **Delete** the message from the conversation

Code blocks in an answer have a **Copy code** button that copies only the code.

## Message versions

When you edit a message or regenerate a response, Saga creates a new version instead of overwriting the original. Navigation arrows appear so you can move between versions.

This is useful when you want to explore different angles on the same question — for example, asking the AI to analyze a clause under two different jurisdictions, or rephrasing a question to see whether a broader or narrower framing surfaces better results. Each version is preserved, so you can always go back.

## Search in a chat

To find a word in a long conversation, click **Search** in the chat header, or press **Cmd+F** (Mac) or **Ctrl+F** (Windows) while the chat is open. Type your term. Saga highlights every match in your prompts and in the answers, and shows a counter. Press **Enter** for the next match and **Shift+Enter** for the previous one, or use the arrows. Saga scrolls each match into view.

Text inside collapsed sections, such as thinking steps, is searched only after you open them. Press **Cmd+F** or **Ctrl+F** again inside the search field to use your browser's own find.

## Tasks

Tasks are built-in commands for common operations. Access them from the **Tasks** button in the chat toolbar or type `/` in the input:

| Task | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| `/summarize` | Generate a concise summary of the provided text or documents |
| `/anonymize` | Redact personal identifiers and sensitive information |
| `/proofread` | Check text for grammar, spelling, and style improvements |
| `/compare` | Compare two texts or document sections and highlight differences |

Select a task from the dropdown, then provide the text or documents to process.

## Improve prompt

Before sending a message, click the pen icon next to the send button to let the AI refine your prompt. This can help you get more targeted results, especially for complex questions. If you prefer your original text, click the revert arrow to undo the improvement.

## Voice input

Click the microphone icon in the chat input to dictate your message instead of typing. Saga transcribes what you say into the input box, where you can edit it before sending. Recording is capped at thirty minutes per session and requires microphone access in your browser.

Voice transcription is powered by Deepgram. The language Deepgram listens for is tied to your Saga interface language — set it under **Settings > Personalization > Default language**. Supported languages are English, Norwegian, Dutch, Danish, Polish, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, French, and Swedish. If the UI is set to a language outside this list, Saga falls back to English transcription.

If you dictate in Polish but Saga's interface is in English, the transcript will be garbled — Deepgram is listening for the wrong language. Switch the UI language to match the language you're speaking.

## Thinking mode

Some models show their step-by-step reasoning in an expandable section before the final answer. This is useful for complex legal questions where you want to see how the AI arrived at its conclusion. If you don't need the reasoning, click **Skip thinking for a faster answer** to get a direct response.

## When the AI needs a decision

If a request is ambiguous in a way that would change the work, the AI can pause and ask you a structured question instead of guessing. The question replaces the message input until you answer.

Choose one of the offered options, or type a short reply. Use **Skip** if you want the AI to continue without that decision. After you send an answer, the normal input comes back. Answered questions stay in the thread as a compact summary; skipped questions stay marked as skipped.

## Email drafts

Ask Saga to write an email and it shows an email card in the chat with **To**, **Cc**, **Subject**, **From**, and **Body**. Click any field to edit it. Paste several addresses to add them as separate recipients.

Choose **Send** to open the email in your mail client, ready to review and send from there. Saga cannot send the email itself, and your mail client chooses the sending account. If the email is too long for a mail link, Saga opens the email without the body and copies the body to your clipboard, so you can paste it in. Choose **Discard** if you do not need it. The card then shows **Sent** or **Discarded**.

You can ask Saga to change the draft in your next message. Email cards are available on the platform, not in the Word or Outlook add-in.

When you ask Saga to reply to an email from the conversation, the subject starts with `Re:` and the original subject. Saga cannot link the reply to the original thread in your mail client. When Saga copies the body to your clipboard, the paragraph breaks stay. **Export to Word** on the answer, and the export of the full chat, include the email's **To**, **Cc**, and **Subject** lines and the body.

## Export a conversation

To download an entire chat as a Word document, click the export button in the chat header. The export includes all messages, citations, and the conversation title. You can also export individual messages from the message action menu.

## AI-generated documents

When the AI drafts a document — such as a memo, summary, or contract clause — it appears as a chip below the response. Click the chip to open the document in the side panel. Hover over it and click the download icon to export it as a Word file.

When an answer has two or more finished drafts, click **Download All as Word (N)** under the draft chips to download one `Saga Drafts.zip` with a Word file for each draft. Each file uses your firm's document styling.

Drafts keep their citations when you download them as Word, PDF, or UDF, and when you copy them. Each cited passage gets a superscript number, and a **Citation sources** list at the end of the document names the sources. Statute and case references that the draft names in the text stay in the text.

When you edit a draft in the side panel, use **Text color** in the toolbar to color text (Automatic, red, orange, green, blue, purple, or gray). The color stays in Word and PDF downloads. Use **Insert image**, or paste an image, to add a PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP image to the draft. The version menu labels each version by its number, the same number the chat shows, and marks the version that is open.

## The side panel

When a document is open in the side panel beside the chat, drag the divider between the chat and the panel to make the document wider or narrower. The chat keeps the rest of the width. The panel does not get narrower than its default width, and the chat always keeps enough room to stay usable. Double-click the divider to go back to the default width.

When you open a Word (`.docx`) file in the side panel, the preview uses that file's own fonts, heading sizes, headers, and footers, including logos in the header and footer. You see the styled document without downloading it first.

## Editing a document with AI

You can rewrite a passage in an AI-generated document without starting over. Open the document from its chip, select the text, and click **Insert to Chat**. The selection appears as a card in the composer. Describe the change and send. Saga creates a new document version with that section updated.

You can add up to five selections from the same document version and edit them with one instruction. Click a card later to reopen the document with that passage highlighted.

Selections that contain a table cannot be edited with AI yet. Overlapping selections, a sixth selection, or text from a different document version are blocked. Remove attached files, a task, or a selected workflow from the message before inserting text.

## AI permissions

Open **Settings > AI Permissions** to choose what Saga may do on its own in your chats. Each row has three choices: **Ask**, **Allow**, or **Off**.

| Row | What it covers | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Web Search | Search the public internet | Ask |
| Deep Web Search | Longer, deeper web research | Ask |
| Legal Sources | Cite authorities from the legal databases enabled for your workspace | Ask |
| Internal Sources | Use files from the organizational sources you select | Ask |
| MCPs | Use the tools of your connected MCP servers | Ask |
| Drafting | Generate and revise documents | Allow |
| Workflow Generation | Build a workflow for your task | Allow |
| Questioner | Ask you clarifying questions in chat | Allow (no Ask option) |

With **Ask**, Saga stops before it uses that feature and shows a card in the chat: "Saga wants to use … for this request". Choose **Allow** to continue once, **Always Allow** to switch that row to Allow, or **Skip** to continue without it. When you turn a source on in the composer yourself, Saga does not ask again for that message. With **Off**, Saga never uses that feature. Your choices save to your account.

Below the web search rows, Saga shows the same warning as in chat: "Web Search is active. Do not upload any confidential information or personal data. Use this mode for general research." If your workspace has turned off web search or deep web search, that row does not show.

The Drafting row replaces the old Auto drafting switch next to the message input. With Drafting off, Saga answers in the chat and does not create documents or presentations.

## Chat text size

Open your account menu (your avatar in the sidebar). Under the theme control, choose **Default** or **Compact** for **Chat text size**. Compact makes the chat thread denser, so long conversations are easier to scan. The setting applies only to the chat thread. It does not change documents, grids, or workflows. On phones, Default and Compact look the same. Your choice saves to your account right away.

## Chat history

Your conversations are saved in the chat history. The sidebar shows your most recent chats under **Chats**, and the chats you added to favorites under **Favorites**. The **Favorites** section shows only when you have a favorite chat. The list includes your chats in projects as well as your other chats.

Open the actions menu on a chat to add it to favorites, rename it, move it to a project with **Change Project**, remove it from its project, or delete it.

To see every chat, click **View All**, or click the **Chats** or **Favorites** heading. This opens **All chats**. Click the search icon next to **Chats** to open **All chats** with the search field ready. In **All chats** you can:

- Search your chats.
- Filter by project or by visibility status (**Shared** or **Private**), and sort by newest or oldest. **Clear Filters** removes the filters.
- Click **Select** and tick chats, or click **Select All** to select every chat that matches your search and filters, up to 500 chats. Then click **Move to Project**, **Export**, or **Delete**.

**Export** downloads one `chat-export.zip` file with a Word document for each selected chat.

Chats are scoped to the account and workspace that created them. If a chat seems to be missing, check that you're signed in to the same workspace (firms with multiple Saga subdomains have separate chat lists per subdomain). See [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting) for more recovery steps.

When your workspace deletes old chats under its data retention setting, Saga warns you before a chat goes. The chat history row, the project chat card, and the open chat show **Expires in N days** or **Expires in N hours**. The warning starts 14 days before deletion for 3, 6 and 12 month settings, 7 days before for 1 month, 2 days before for 1 week, and at once for 24 hours. Admins can click the label to open the data retention settings.

## How long chats are handled

You don't need to manage the AI's context window manually. The AI fetches document content on demand through tool calls rather than holding every document in its prompt, so long conversations no longer hit a hard percentage cutoff and there's no "context full" warning in current versions of Saga.

Very long chats can still feel slower because the AI has more history to read. If you're starting a new line of analysis or moving to a different document set, open a fresh chat — it's faster and keeps the focus tight. For ongoing work over days or weeks, upload your documents to a project's [knowledge base](/guides/projects/knowledge-base) instead of re-attaching them in each new chat.
