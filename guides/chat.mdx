Chat is Saga's conversational AI interface. Ask questions in natural language, attach documents for context, and choose from built-in or custom assistants.

## Start a conversation

1. Click **Chat** in the sidebar.
2. Type your question in the message input.
3. Press **Enter** or click send.

You can also start a chat from within a [matter](/guides/matters) to automatically scope the conversation to that matter's knowledge base.

## Assistants

Saga provides built-in assistants and supports custom ones created by your organization.

### Switch assistants

Click the assistant selector at the top of the chat to choose a different assistant. Each assistant may have different capabilities and instructions.

### Custom assistants

Admins can create custom assistants with specific instructions, tone, and focus areas. See [Admin panel](/guides/admin-panel) for setup details.

## Add context

### Upload documents

Click the attachment icon to upload files directly into a conversation. The AI uses these documents to answer your questions.

### Use project documents

When chatting inside a matter, the knowledge base documents are automatically available. You can also select specific documents from your matter to focus the conversation.

## Features

### Web search

Toggle **Web search** to let the AI search the web alongside your documents when answering questions.

### Prompt library

Access saved prompts by clicking the prompt library icon. Use pre-built prompts or create your own for common tasks.

### Chat history

View past conversations in the sidebar. Filter by:

- **All** — every conversation
- **Private** — your personal chats
- **Shared** — conversations shared with your team

### Message branching

Click on a previous message to branch the conversation from that point, exploring different directions without losing the original thread.

## Citations

The AI includes citations that reference specific passages in your documents. Click a citation to view the source in the **Instruments sheet** side panel.
