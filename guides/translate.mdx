Saga's translation feature helps you translate legal documents and text between languages. Powered by DeepL, it supports 30+ languages — including regional variants like British and American English — and preserves document formatting during translation.

## Translate text or documents

Saga offers two translation modes:

- **[Text translation](/guides/translate/text)** — Paste or type text and get an instant translation
- **[Document translation](/guides/translate/documents)** — Upload a document and download the translated version with formatting preserved

## Supported languages

Saga supports over 30 languages through DeepL, including regional variants such as EN-GB, EN-US, and ES-419 (Latin American Spanish). The source language can be auto-detected or set manually.

<Tip>
For legal documents with jurisdiction-specific terminology, specify the source language manually rather than relying on auto-detection. This produces more accurate translations.
</Tip>

## Swap languages

Click the swap button between the source and target language selectors to quickly reverse the translation direction.

## Service status

A status indicator shows whether the translation service is available. If the service is temporarily offline, Saga queues your request and retries automatically when the connection is restored.
