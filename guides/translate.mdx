Saga's translation feature helps you translate legal documents and text between languages while preserving formatting and legal terminology.

## Supported languages

Saga supports translation between a wide range of languages. The source language can be auto-detected, or you can specify it manually for better accuracy with specialized legal terminology.

<Tip>
For best results with legal documents, specify the source language rather than relying on auto-detection. This helps the AI handle jurisdiction-specific terminology more accurately.
</Tip>
