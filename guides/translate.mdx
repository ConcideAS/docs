title: Translate
description: Translate documents and text between languages with AI-powered translation.
---

Saga's translation feature helps you translate legal documents and text between languages while preserving formatting and legal terminology.

## Translate text

1. Click **Translate** in the sidebar.
2. Type or paste text into the source field.
3. Select the **source language** (or use auto-detect).
4. Select the **target language**.
5. Click **Translate**.

## Translate documents

1. Click the upload area to select a file, or drag and drop.
2. Saga supports multiple formats including PDF, Word, and text files.
3. Choose your target language and click **Translate**.

You can also select documents from your matters or from **Epona365** using the document picker.

## Supported languages

Saga supports translation between a wide range of languages. The source language can be auto-detected, or you can specify it manually for better accuracy with specialized legal terminology.

<Tip>
For best results with legal documents, specify the source language rather than relying on auto-detection. This helps the AI handle jurisdiction-specific terminology more accurately.
</Tip>
