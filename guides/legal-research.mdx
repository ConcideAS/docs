title: Legal research
description: Search across legal databases to find case law, legislation, and legal commentary.
---

Legal research lets you search multiple legal databases from a single interface. Find relevant case law, legislation, and commentary, then add results directly to your matters.

## Search

1. Click **Legal research** in the sidebar.
2. Enter your search query — use natural language or specific legal terms.
3. Press **Enter** to search.

## Databases

Saga connects to multiple legal databases:

| Database | Coverage |
|----------|---------|
| **Lovdata** | Norwegian legislation and case law |
| **Rettsdata** | Norwegian legal commentary and analysis |
| **SDU GenIA-L** | Danish legal resources |
| **NL (Rechtspraak)** | Dutch court decisions |
| **Moonlit** | EU legislation and case law |
| **Kennisbank** | Legal knowledge base |

<Note>
Available databases depend on your organization's subscriptions and configuration.
</Note>

## Search modes

- **Prompt search** — describe what you're looking for in natural language
- **Keyword search** — use specific legal terms and references
- **Hybrid search** — combines both approaches for broader results

## Filter results

Narrow your results using filters:

- **Institutions** — filter by court or regulatory body
- **Jurisdictions** — limit to specific jurisdictions
- **Document types** — focus on case law, legislation, or commentary

## Add results to a matter

Select one or more results and click **Add to matter** to save them to a matter's knowledge base. This makes them available as context for AI chat and grid reviews within that matter.
