Legal research lets you search multiple legal databases without leaving Saga. Find relevant case law, legislation, and commentary, then add results directly to your projects for further analysis in chat or grid review.

<Tip>
Search for recent case law on data breach liability, then add the most relevant decisions to your project for further analysis in chat or grid review.
</Tip>

## Search

1. Click **Legal research** in the sidebar.
2. Select the database you want to search.
3. Enter your query — you can use natural language or specific legal terms.
4. Press **Enter** to search.

## View results

Results appear in a list with key metadata. Click on any result to preview the full document in Saga's [document viewer](/guides/projects/document-viewer).

For each result, you can:

- **View** the full document in the side panel
- **Copy citation** to your clipboard
- **Add to project** to save it to a project's knowledge base
- **Dismiss** to remove it from the results list

## Add results to a project

Select one or more results and click **Add to project** to save them to a project's knowledge base. You can choose which folder to save them to. Once added, these documents are available as context for AI chat and grid reviews within that project.

To add all results at once, use the **bulk add** option.
