description: Search across legal databases to find case law, legislation, and legal commentary.
---

Legal research lets you search multiple legal databases from a single interface. Find relevant case law, legislation, and commentary, then add results directly to your projects.

## Search

1. Click **Legal research** in the sidebar.
2. Enter your search query — use natural language or specific legal terms.
3. Press **Enter** to search.

## Add results to a project

Select one or more results and click **Add to project** to save them to a project's knowledge base. This makes them available as context for AI chat and grid reviews within that project.
