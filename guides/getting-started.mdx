This guide walks you through signing in, navigating the interface, and starting your first project.

## Sign in

1. Go to your organization's Saga URL or [app.saga.com](https://app.saga.com).
2. Sign in with your credentials.
3. You land on the **Chat** page, ready to start working.

## Navigate the interface

Saga's sidebar gives you access to every feature:

| Section | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Chat** | AI conversations with optional document context |
| **Projects** | Workspaces for organizing cases, documents, and reviews |
| **Legal research** | Search across legal databases |
| **Translate** | Translate documents and text |
| **Workflows** | Visual automation builder |
| **Admin panel** | Organization settings (admins only) |

## Create your first project

A **project** is a workspace for a case or project. It keeps your documents, chats, and grid reviews organized in one place.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects">
    Click **Projects** in the sidebar, then click **New project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your project">
    Give it a descriptive name, such as the case name or project reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documents">
    Upload files to the project's **Knowledge base**. These documents become context for AI conversations and grid reviews.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working">
    Use the **Chat** tab to ask questions about your documents, or create a **Grid review** to analyze them systematically.
  </Step>
</Steps>
