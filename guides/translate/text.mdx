---
title: Text translation
description: Translate text between languages with instant results.
---

Text translation lets you paste or type content and see the translation appear in real time.

## Translate text

1. Click **Translate** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Text** tab.
3. Choose a source language or leave it on **Auto-detect**.
4. Select the target language.
5. Paste or type your text in the left panel.
6. The translation appears automatically in the right panel.

Translation updates as you type, with a short delay to avoid unnecessary processing.

## Copy the translation

Click the **Copy** button in the output panel to copy the translated text to your clipboard.
