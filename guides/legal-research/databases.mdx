title: Databases
---

Saga integrates with multiple legal databases. The databases available to you depend on your organization's configuration and jurisdiction.

## Available databases

| Database | Content | Jurisdiction |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Rettsdata** | Case law, legislation, and legal commentary | Norway |
| **Lovdata** | Legislation, regulations, and court decisions | Norway |
| **SDU GenIA-L** | Legal research and analysis | Denmark |
| **Rechtspraak.nl** | Court decisions | Netherlands |
| **Wetten** | Legislation and regulations | Netherlands |
| **Moonlit** | Case law across multiple jurisdictions | Multi-jurisdiction |
| **EurLex** | EU legislation, case law, and treaties | European Union |
| **Kennisbank** | Tax law knowledge base | Netherlands |

<Note>
Not all databases are available in every Saga instance. Your organization's administrators configure which databases are enabled. If you need access to a database that isn't available, contact your administrator.
</Note>

## Database-specific features

Some databases offer additional capabilities:

- **Moonlit** provides filtering by fields of law, document types, jurisdictions, and portals
- **Lovdata** includes document type filters (e.g., law gazette, regulations)
- **Kennisbank** supports filtering by tax type
- **Rettsdata** and **Rechtspraak.nl** offer institution and jurisdiction filters

See [Search modes and filters](/guides/legal-research/search-and-filters) for details on how to use these options.
