---
title: Databases
description: Legal databases available in Saga for case law, legislation, and commentary.
---

Saga connects to multiple legal databases:

| Database | Coverage |
|----------|---------|
| **Lovdata** | Norwegian legislation and case law |
| **Rettsdata** | Norwegian legal commentary and analysis |
| **SDU GenIA-L** | Danish legal resources |
| **NL (Rechtspraak)** | Dutch court decisions |
| **Moonlit** | EU legislation and case law |
| **Kennisbank** | Legal knowledge base |

<Note>
Available databases depend on your organization's subscriptions and configuration.
</Note>
