## Search modes

- **Prompt search** — describe what you're looking for in natural language
- **Keyword search** — use specific legal terms and references
- **Hybrid search** — combines both approaches for broader results

## Filter results

Narrow your results using filters:

- **Institutions** — filter by court or regulatory body
- **Jurisdictions** — limit to specific jurisdictions
- **Document types** — focus on case law, legislation, or commentary
