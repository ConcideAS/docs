The Saga Outlook add-in brings Saga into Microsoft Outlook. You can manage matter context, save correspondence, and generate AI-assisted drafts without switching applications.

The add-in is anchored in the project's knowledge base. Every AI action can draw on selected documents, writing style, and correspondence history.

## What you can do

The add-in opens as a task pane inside Outlook. From there, you can save emails and attachments to Saga, bring project documents into drafts, and work with Saga AI while an email is open.

### Save document to project

When you are reading an email with an attachment, contract, report, or signed agreement, you can send it straight to a Saga project without leaving Outlook. Select the attachment, choose a destination project and folder, and Saga saves it to the project's knowledge base.

- Supports any file type that Saga accepts
- Lets future AI actions on the matter reference the document
- Removes the need to download and re-upload files

### Save thread to project

Important exchanges with a client or counterparty should live alongside your documents, not only in your inbox. With one action, you can save the entire thread to a Saga project as a structured document.

- Preserves sender details, dates, full message bodies, and attachments
- Makes the thread part of the AI context
- Lets summaries, drafts, and searches draw from the thread automatically

### Import document from project into email

While composing an email, you can browse or search the project knowledge base directly from the task pane. You do not need to search through a file server or your desktop.

- Finds precedents, clauses, or documents in seconds
- Attaches the file to your draft or inserts text into the email body
- Scopes results to the selected project, so they stay relevant

### Summarise thread

Long threads accumulate quickly. Instead of scrolling through weeks of replies, you can generate a clear, structured summary of where things stand.

- Covers key points, open action items, and each party's position
- Includes a chronological timeline of how the conversation developed
- Flags connections to project documents when the thread is linked to a project

### Draft reply with thread context

When you need to respond to an email, give the AI a short instruction and it produces a draft grounded in the thread and the selected Saga project.

- Works from a brief prompt like "confirm the revised timeline" or "decline politely"
- Matches the writing style of your existing matter correspondence
- Lets you review the draft in the task pane, insert it into your reply, and send

### Find and understand correspondence with a person

Before a call, negotiation, or new instruction, you can search by name or email address to view the history of interactions with that contact across your matters.

- Surfaces saved threads and documents that reference the person
- Displays results as a timeline, so you can see the relationship at a glance
- Helps with client preparation, due diligence, and matter-file review

## Saga AI chat

The full Saga AI chat is available in the task pane at any time. You can ask free-form questions, run document searches, or use any Saga AI tool while an email is open.
