The Saga Outlook add-in brings Saga into Microsoft Outlook. .
## What you can do

The add-in opens inside Outlook. From there, you can save emails and attachments to Saga, bring project documents into drafts, summarize thread and draft a reply .

### Save thread to project

When you save an email thread to a Saga project, the entire conversation (including all messages in the thread) gets linked to a project in Saga. This means:

- The email becomes part of the project's record, alongside documents, notes, and other files.
- Anyone with access to the project can see the thread without having to forward emails or dig through inboxes.
- The thread is searchable and accessible directly from the project in Saga, rather than buried in your email client.
It's essentially a way to bring relevant email correspondence into your legal matter workspace so everything is in one place.

### Save attachment to a project

When you save attachments to a project, you're moving files from an email (or chat) into the project's knowledge base. This action:

- They move from a single email thread or chat into the project's document collection.
- Once in the knowledge base, the files become accessible to any chat within that project, grid reviews in that same project
- Knowledge base documents remain stored long-term and can be used for multiple questions and analyses without re-attaching

### Summarize thread

Long threads accumulate quickly. Instead of scrolling through weeks of replies, you can generate a clear, structured summary of where things stand.

- Covers key points, organize them chronologically, and rewrite them into a clean, easy-to-read bulleted list

### Draft a reply 

Saga analyze the content of the current email thread you have open in Outlook, notice the context, the sender, and the subject line.

- It generate a professional and concise response based on the thread's context (or any specific instructions you give me, such as "Draft a reply saying I approve").
- When is ready and yoy Insert reply, the drafted text is automatically populated into your Outlook email composer, complete with the correct "To" and "Cc" recipients and subject line.
The email is not sent automatically. You always get to review, edit, and adjust the draft in Outlook before you hit send.
