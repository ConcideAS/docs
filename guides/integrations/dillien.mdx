---
title: Dillien
---

The Dillien integration lets you import documents from Dillien data rooms directly into Saga. If your firm uses Dillien for deal or matter files, you can browse and select documents without leaving the platform.

## Where you can use it

The Dillien option appears anywhere you add documents in Saga:

- **Knowledge base** — click **Upload** and select **Upload from Dillien**
- **Chat** — click the attach button and select **Upload from Dillien**
- **Grid review** — click **Add documents** and select **Upload from Dillien**

## Browsing and selecting files

When you open the Dillien picker, you sign in with your Dillien account and browse the data rooms you have access to. You can select documents from those rooms and bring them into Saga.

Documents are copied into Saga as a one-way import. Changes you make in Saga don't sync back to Dillien, and updates in Dillien don't automatically appear in Saga. If you need an updated version, import it again.

## Setup

Dillien is configured by your admin from the [admin panel](/guides/admin) under **Settings > Integrations**. Your admin enables the integration. Once it's on, everyone in your organization can connect their Dillien account when they import files.

If you don't see the Dillien option when uploading documents, ask your admin to enable it.
