## 1. Sign in

Go to your organization's Saga URL or [app.saga.com](https://app.saga.com) and sign in with your credentials.

## 2. Create a project

Click **Projects** in the sidebar, then click **New project**. Give it a name that matches your case or project.

## 3. Upload documents

Add files to your project's **Knowledge base**. These documents become context for AI conversations and reviews.

## 4. Start asking questions

Open **Chat** and ask questions about your documents. Saga's AI uses your uploaded files as context to give accurate, sourced answers.
