title: Quickstart
description: Go from sign-in to your first AI-assisted task in under five minutes.
---

This guide walks you through the fastest path to getting value from Saga — sign in, create a project, upload a document, and ask AI a question about it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to your organization's Saga URL or [app.saga.com](https://app.saga.com) and sign in with your email or Microsoft account. You'll land on the **Chat** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Click **Projects** in the sidebar, then click **New project**. Give it a descriptive name — for example, the case name or client reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload documents">
    Open your project and go to the **Knowledge base** tab. Drag and drop files into the upload area, or click to browse. Saga supports PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and many other formats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question">
    Switch to the **Chat** tab inside your project. Type a question about your documents — for example, "What are the key termination provisions in these contracts?" Press **Enter** to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review citations">
    The AI response includes numbered citations that reference specific passages in your documents. Click any citation to open the source document in the side panel and verify the answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>
