A **matter** is a workspace that brings together everything related to a case or project: documents, AI conversations, and structured document reviews.

## Create a matter

1. Click **Matters** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New matter**.
3. Enter a name for the matter.

## Knowledge base

The knowledge base is where you store documents that the AI uses as context when answering questions.

### Upload documents

1. Open a matter and go to the **Knowledge base** tab.
2. Drag and drop files or click to browse.
3. Saga supports common document formats including PDF, Word, and text files.

### Use documents as context

Once uploaded, documents in the knowledge base are automatically available as context when you:

- Chat within the matter
- Run a grid review in the matter

## Matter chat

Each matter has its own **Chat** tab. Conversations here are scoped to the matter's knowledge base, so the AI answers based on your uploaded documents.

See [Chat](/guides/chat) for full details on AI conversations.

## Grid review

Create structured reviews directly from a matter. Go to the **Grid review** tab to set up a question grid against your documents.

See [Grid review](/guides/grid-review) for full details.

## Manage matters

- **Rename**: Click the matter name to edit it.
- **Delete**: Open matter settings and select **Delete matter**.
- **Share**: Matters are visible to your organization by default. Check with your admin for access controls.
