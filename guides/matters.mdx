A **project** is a workspace that brings together everything related to a case or project: documents, AI conversations, and structured document reviews.

## Create a project

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New project**.
3. Enter a name for the project.

## Project chat

Each project has its own **Chat** tab. Conversations here are scoped to the project's knowledge base, so the AI answers based on your uploaded documents.

See [Chat](/guides/chat) for full details on AI conversations.

## Grid review

Create structured reviews directly from a project. Go to the **Grid review** tab to set up a question grid against your documents.

See [Grid review](/guides/grid-review) for full details.
