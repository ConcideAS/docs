---
title: Data deletion
---

This page lists what each deletion action removes, what it leaves, and who controls it. Use it when you set retention, remove a user, or ask what happens when a contract ends.

Saga does not offer a per-user hard purge that erases one person's data while the workspace stays active. Removing a user deactivates the account. It does not wipe that person's history from the workspace.

## Deletion actions

| Action | Who controls it | Removes | Leaves | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace retention | An admin, in **Data Retention** | Chats, project chats, grid reviews, legal research searches, and project activity | Documents and other project data | Choose a time frame below. Items older than the time frame are deleted after a 24-hour grace period. |
| Remove a user | An admin, in **Users** | That person's access. The account is deactivated. | The account. Chats and projects stay associated with it. An admin can reactivate it. | On remove. Reactivation brings the same account back. |
| Delete a document | A person deleting a file in a project knowledge base | That document | The rest of the project | Undo is available for a few seconds (about five). After that, the delete is permanent. |
| Delete a project | A person who can delete that project (owner or co-owner) | Documents, chats, and grid reviews in the project. Collaborators lose access immediately. | The rest of the workspace | Permanent. Steps are on [Projects](/sv/guides/projects). |
| Contract end | Saga, after the contract ends. There is no admin-panel control. | Tenant data within 60 days. Backups within 36 days. | Not a per-user action | Platform logs are kept for 12 months. |

## Workspace retention

Admins set retention in the [admin panel](/sv/guides/administration/admin-panel) under **Data Retention**.

Unlimited is the default. With Unlimited, Saga does not delete activity on a schedule. The other values are 24 hours, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, and 12 months.

When retention will remove a chat soon, Saga shows an expiry warning on the chat. The warning starts 14 days before deletion for 3, 6, and 12 month settings, 7 days before for 1 month, 2 days before for 1 week, and at once for 24 hours. See [Chat](/sv/guides/chat#chat-history).

## Removing a user does not erase their data

When an admin removes a user, Saga deactivates the account. The person cannot sign in. Their chats and projects stay with the account. An admin can reactivate the account from **Users** in the [admin panel](/sv/guides/administration/admin-panel). See [Troubleshooting](/sv/guides/troubleshooting) for returning colleagues.

There is no product control that erases one user's data while the workspace stays live.

## When a contract ends

When a Saga contract ends, Saga deletes the tenant's data within **60 days**. Backups of that data are purged within **36 days**. Platform logs are kept for **12 months**.

Contract-end deletion is workspace-wide. It is not a way to erase one user.
